# Healthy aging

Prioritize function, independence, well-being, and sustainable habits over promises to reverse aging. Ask which limitation or habit matters most now and choose one realistic action with a review date. Use the nutrition, training, and medical modules for detailed decisions.

## Lifestyle priorities

- **Social connection:** support meaningful relationships, friendships, and a community suited to the person's interests, values, and desired level of contact. Favor manageable recurring contact or shared activities; respect chosen solitude and avoid imposing a social quota.
- **Movement:** encourage regular activity, strength, balance, and mobility adapted to capacity. A modest safe session counts; consistency matters more than a perfect program.
- **Tobacco:** recommend not smoking and support cessation. Moderation is not a tobacco safety strategy.
- **Alcohol and drinks:** favor less alcohol; not drinking is a valid choice, and alcohol should not be recommended for longevity. Favor water and limit sugary sodas. Avoid implying that a low alcohol intake is risk-free.
- **Food:** use the food-first and flexible eating approach in [nutrition-and-weight.md](nutrition-and-weight.md), with ordinary meals preferred over unnecessary sports products.
- **Sleep:** encourage adequate, regular, restorative sleep and assess persistent sleep problems through the medical workflow rather than promising a supplement solution.
- **Body composition:** interpret weight relative to height, muscle, waist measurements, function, and medical context. Preserve strength and muscle with age while addressing excess adiposity when appropriate. Avoid arbitrary targets for "not too much muscle," appearance-based judgments, or treating BMI alone as a diagnosis. Extreme leanness, excessive bulking, performance-enhancing drugs, and restrictive diets are not longevity goals.

Keep discretionary foods flexible; safety and medical restrictions still apply. Prioritize accessible habits over expensive products and avoid unnecessary peptides. Use [medicines-and-supplements.md](medicines-and-supplements.md) for Epitalon or other anti-aging products; lifestyle coaching does not replace indicated treatment or preventive care.
